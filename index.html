Our Heritage & Ethos

A Sanctuary for Curiosity, Speculation & Rigor

Serving readers across Veyangoda and Sri Lanka, Gaya.Library bridges centuries of literature with cutting-edge open science and engineering. We believe intellectual discovery should be transparent, accessible, and fine-free.
